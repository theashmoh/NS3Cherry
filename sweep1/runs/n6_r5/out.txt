| NR-NTN VOICE CELL                                 transparent, NTN-Rural, 5 MHz FDD, DL 2.175 / UL 1.985 GHz, 6 downlink calls |

VOICE
  source          RTP, codec 4.75 kbit/s = 95 bits per 20 ms frame: 12 B voice frames
                  (the bits of the frame alone; with the 10-bit payload header of RFC 4867 it is 14 B: --voicePayload=14)
  silence (DTX)   7 more voice frames (hangover), then a 7 B SID at once and every 160 ms
  headers         IP 20 + UDP 8 + RTP 12 = 40 B on every packet
  ROHC            size table: UO-0 3 B, UOR-2-EXT-3 13 B, IR 39 B (1 at the start)
  ROHC context    over 13 packets missing in a row: needs UOR-2-EXT-3 or IR; over 61: needs IR
                  (UO-0 has 4 sequence number bits, UOR-2 6; with p = 1, RFC 3095 5.7, that is 2^4 - 3 and 2^6 - 3)
  ROHC repair     O-mode: NACK or STATIC-NACK on the reverse link at the first packet it cannot restore, again every 32
                  such packets (none for lost packets the window covers; RFC 3095 5.3.2.2.3, k_1 of the last n_1 = 1 of 1)
  layer 2         PDCP 1 + RLC 1 + MAC 3 = 5 B, as TR 26.940 5.2.3 counts them; NR has the same 5 B as PDCP 2 + RLC 1 + MAC 2
  below the MAC   voice 12 + 3 + 5 = 20 B, SID 7 + 3 + 5 = 15 B, 10 B more on a context update
  bearer          5QI 1 for every UE: GBR, GFBR 40.00 kbit/s (talking: 20.80), 1 % error rate
  delay budget    100 ms UE-UPF, of which UPF-gateway takes 7.64 ms in this run: 92.36 ms are left for the radio side, gateway-UE
                  (TS 23.501 5.7.3.4 takes a fixed 20 ms off for 5QI 1: --upfGwDelayMinMs=20 --upfGwDelayMaxMs=20)
  late packets    the gNB discards a new packet while its oldest has waited over 92 ms (that budget, in whole ms); a UE never does

CELL
  satellite       600 km, Set-1 LEO-600 of TR 38.821: EIRP 34.00 dBW/MHz = 70.99 dBm, antenna 30.00 dBi,
                  G/T 1.10 dB/K (noise figure 4.28 dB)
  beam            2.00 m aperture: 4.0575 deg wide at 3 dB on the downlink, 4.4461 deg on the uplink carrier
  UEs             UE 0 on the beam centre, the others at random places of the beam area
  beam area       42.5 km north-south x 42.5 km east-west, 1419 km2
  LOS, shadowing  each UE keeps its obstruction and shadowing levels for the whole run
  interference    none: one beam, no other cell; every SINR here is a signal-to-noise ratio
  scheduler       TDMA (a UE gets whole symbols), round robin
  HARQ            up to 4 transmissions of a block, combined by the receiver (incremental redundancy); 16 processes per UE
  link adaptation downlink: a first transmission aims at 15 % block errors (outer loop on the HARQ feedback)
  control         a DCI is lost at the block error rate of its aggregation level at the SINR it arrives with
  link failure    when a level-8 PDCCH with 4 dB more power would lose over 10 % (below -13.82 dB), over the last 200 ms,
                  and a level-4 PDCCH not under 2 % again (above -5.93 dB) within 1.00 s
  after a failure no service until the level-4 PDCCH loses under 2 % again, then the UE attaches again

PDCCH
  CORESET         24 RB x 1, 2 or 3 symbols = 4, 8 or 12 CCEs as the slot needs; a DCI is 34 + 24 CRC bits
                  (format 1_0 on 26 RB has 37 bits, TS 38.212 7.3.1.2.1: --dciBits=37)
  level           the lowest that loses up to 1.00 % of the DCIs at the SINR the UE reported:
                  level 1 from 0.40 dB, 2 from -2.61 dB, 4 from -5.62 dB, 8 from -8.63 dB
                  level-1 BLER, dB:BLER (--pdcchBlerTable): -4:0.9303 -2:0.4356 -1:0.1453 0:0.0246 0.5:0.00797 1:0.00221
  candidates      6/6/4/2 per UE at level 1/2/4/8; SIB1 and random access at level 8; a UE monitors 72 (limit 44)
  order in a slot SIB1 (every 20 ms), random access, uplink grants (50 % of the CCEs if the downlink wants them too),
                  downlink assignments; retransmissions first, then new data as the scheduler ranks the UEs
  voice           dynamic scheduling: every block has a DCI

GEOMETRY AND DELAY
  satellite       flies south at 7.56 km/s, is over UE 0 at t = 255.24 s and down at 10 deg again at t = 509.49 s
  calls           UE 0: t = 1.00 s to 509.49 s, the whole pass; call i starts i x 1 ms after UE 0's,

  UE 0            elevation -10.00 -> +10.00 deg (- before it is overhead, + after), slant range 1931.64 -> 1931.64 km
  gateway         N 25.6 km, E -9.2 km from UE 0 (random in a circle of 200 km diameter)
                  elevation -10.36 -> +9.65 deg, slant range 1907.12 -> 1956.24 km
  feeder link     gateway 60.00 dBm, satellite receive gain 30.00 dBi
  delay to UE 0   32.80 ms = 12.36 host-UPF + 7.64 UPF-gateway + 6.36 feeder + 6.44 service, one way, when its call starts
